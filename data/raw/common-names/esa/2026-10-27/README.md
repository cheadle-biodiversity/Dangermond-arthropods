# ESA common-name source

Downloaded 27 October 2026 from the Entomological Society of America
Common Names of Insects Database:
https://entsoc.org/publications/common-names

The published download filename is `Common_names_list_7-2-26.xlsx`.
Its internal heading says "Updated 3/4/25"; retain both dates as provided
rather than inferring a newer content date. `archive_manifest.json`
records the exact download URL, retrieval date and SHA-256 checksum.
The original spreadsheet is retained unchanged and ignored by Git.

This version uses ESA, not BugGuide, as the common-name source. Only
species-level names are joined; genus/family common labels are not
assigned as species names. Taxonomic aliases are documented in the
analysis workflow and lookup table. Third-party ownership remains with
ESA; no new license is asserted for the source list.
