# First-pass habitat and life-history evidence

All 2,550 unique species in the Level IV 6ap and 85a checklists were screened using their 43,555 regional occurrences. The annotated copies retain every original species.

- 393 species have publisher-supplied habitat descriptions, many describing habitats other than dunes or beaches.
- A keyword screen identifies 8 species with explicit dune mentions and 16 with beach/wrack/seaweed/kelp mentions in those habitat fields; the groups can overlap. These are records of habitat use, not specialist classifications.
- The local maps provide selected dune-vegetation, strand or beach/dune-substrate evidence for 13 regional candidate species. The maps do not cover the full ecoregions.
- 45 candidate species have local Ecdysis species-matched evidence, with site contexts and original habitat descriptions retained separately.
- 13 candidate species have cited habitat and life-history notes from the initial literature review. Other life-history entries remain blank and explicitly unreviewed.

## Interpreting the map evidence

CDFW ds2957 vegetation uses field work from 2012–2017 and predates dune restoration in 2022. USGS SIM 3472 unit Qey combines Holocene beach and dune sand; Qeo is old dune substrate. Map classes describe collection locations and substrate, not species preferences. Coordinates with unknown uncertainty or uncertainty radii reaching a polygon boundary are flagged in the evidence files.

The 308 mapped, species-matched Ecdysis records include 67 at Perko's Beach, 193 explicitly described as backdune, 12 at other beach-associated localities, and 36 with other or unspecified contexts. These are records across the local species inventory, not counts added to the regional GBIF pool. Perko's Beach points fall in mapped Coastal Strand. Explicit backdune points fall in mapped dune scrub or Semi-natural Stands; collection-time descriptions are retained alongside the older map. Local sources may overlap regional GBIF records.
